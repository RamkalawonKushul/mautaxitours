Terms & Conditions

Free cancellations: Full refund if cancelled at least 24 hrs before scheduled time.

Payment methods: Pay online via credit card (link in booking confirmation email).

Child/Toddler Seats: First seat is free (upon request); EUR 10 per extra seat. Mention child's age during booking.

Snorkeling: Bring your own gear or use shared onboard equipment.

Veg Meals: Mention in “special request” section of booking.

Cleaning Fees: Rs 400 charge for vomiting, sand, spills. Keep the vehicle clean for all passengers.

In-Vehicle Rules: No eating, drinking, or smoking (exceptions for medical needs).

Bad Weather: Trips may be canceled due to cyclones or severe weather. Full refund if rescheduling is not possible. No transfers during class 3+ cyclones.

Booking Policy: Bookings must go through our website. Direct bookings with drivers will result in immediate suspension.
