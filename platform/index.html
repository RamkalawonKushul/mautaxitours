A few helpful answers

Before you set off

Clear information at every step of your booking.

Are the tour prices per person or per vehicle?

Private transport prices shown on tour cards are per vehicle for up to four passengers. Ocean activity prices, where listed, are per person. Attraction tickets not listed in the price breakdown are quoted separately.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your request is an enquiry until our team confirms the date, availability and final amount. We will send you the next steps directly.

Can I pay online by card?

We are preparing secure card checkout with SBM. Until the bank activates the merchant gateway, our team will confirm your booking and provide the approved payment method. Never send card numbers, CVV or banking OTPs by WhatsApp.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. Use the transfer request form with your flight number, pickup time, passenger count and luggage details. We will recommend a suitable vehicle and confirm the fare.

What if we are more than four passengers?

Tell us your full group size and luggage needs. Larger vehicle options are available, and our team will confirm the most suitable arrangement and price.

Can I customise a tour?

Yes. Send us your preferred stops, timing and any mobility or family requirements. We can discuss a tailor-made itinerary.
