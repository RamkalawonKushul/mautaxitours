The Chamarel Adventure Tour

Explore Grand Bassin, Chamarel’s Seven Coloured Earth, waterfalls, rum heritage and scenic viewpoints.

About this experience

Discover Mauritius’s south-west and central highlands on a private day tour. Visit Grand Bassin, Alexandra Falls, Black River Gorges, the Seven Coloured Earth, Chamarel Waterfall and the rum distillery, with scenic views along the way.

Suggested itinerary

  1. Trou aux Cerfs Volcano Crater
  2. Grand Bassin (Ganga Talao)
  3. Alexandra Falls & Black River Gorges
  4. Seven Coloured Earth of Chamarel
  5. Chamarel Waterfall
  6. Rhumerie de Chamarel
  7. Le Morne viewpoint
