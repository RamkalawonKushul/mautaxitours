The current listed capacities are car and SUV up to 4, family van up to 7, standard minivan up to 10, executive minivan up to 14 and coaster or bus up to 22 passengers.

Passenger capacity alone does not determine luggage space. Please include suitcases, equipment, child seats, pickup points, stops and return arrangements so we can confirm a suitable vehicle.

For groups above 22 passengers, contact us for a multiple-vehicle quotation. Vehicle availability and final pricing are confirmed individually.
