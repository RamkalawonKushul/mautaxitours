## Questions and answers

**Do you provide airport transfers in Mauritius?**  
Yes. Send your flight number, arrival date and time, destination, passenger count and luggage details so the team can recommend a suitable vehicle and confirm the fare.

**Can you arrange private tours around Mauritius?**  
Yes. Share the places you would like to visit, your pickup point, travel date and group size. The team can discuss a suitable route and vehicle.

**Are tour prices per person or per vehicle?**  
Private transport prices are quoted by vehicle and group size. Activity tickets and attraction admission may be charged separately per person. Confirm the final breakdown before payment.

**When is a booking confirmed?**  
An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. The team must confirm availability, itinerary and final price directly.

**Can I pay online by card?**  
Do not promise live online card checkout until the SBM merchant gateway has been approved, activated and tested. Until then, explain the actual payment method confirmed by the team. Never request card numbers, CVV or banking OTPs through chat.
